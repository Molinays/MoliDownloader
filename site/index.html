Features

Three tools, one window

Go from a link to the finished file without opening anything else.

01 · Download

Find the video, wherever it is

1000+ sites handled by yt-dlp. If the page is not recognized, the native resolver looks for the video file in the HTML, in player JSON, and on the network: no FetchV-style extensions needed.

  • 4K / 1440p / 1080p video and MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC, WAV audio
  • Public TikTok even without logging in
  • Playlists, subtitles, cookies, proxy, SponsorBlock
  • Up to 8 parallel downloads
  • Automatic retry with browser cookies and stream discovery
  • Spotify playlists, albums, and tracks matched to audio
02 · Editor

Quick finishing touches

Trim, color, rotate, and join multiple clips in sequence. Preview is instant; export uses the same values in FFmpeg.

  • Frame-accurate trim with sliders and numeric fields
  • Brightness, contrast, saturation, gamma, temperature, and presets
  • Rotate, flip, zoom, speed, and volume with fades
  • PNG snapshot and full-timeline preview
  • Export MP4, MKV, or audio only, including 9:16
03 · Compress

Smaller files, same quality

A HandBrake-style compressor that actually tests your machine's GPU instead of guessing.

  • x264, x265, VP9, AV1, and hardware NVENC, Quick Sync, AMF, VideoToolbox
  • Real encoder tests and GPU decoding
  • Live final-size estimate and target size
  • Audio track merge, trim, auto crop, loudnorm
  • Subtitle burn-in and faststart MP4

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install FFmpeg or yt-dlp?

No. FFmpeg, ffprobe, and yt-dlp are included, and the app can update yt-dlp itself from the Download tab.

Can I download a TikTok video without logging in?

Yes, for public videos. If the page does not expose the file, the app opens the public embedded player and captures the mp4. Private or restricted content needs browser cookies, configurable under Access and network.

Does it work on sites that hide the player?

The resolver reads the page, follows iframes, and looks for HLS playlists and mp4 files in the player data. If the site only builds the stream at runtime, a hidden Electron window captures it like an extension would. It does not decrypt DRM or bypass subscriptions.

How does the app update?

The installer version checks releases at startup and every 6 hours, downloads in the background, and installs on exit. The Info tab shows the status and the Restart and update button. The portable version must be replaced by hand.

Does the app send my files anywhere?

No. Downloads, editing, and compression run locally. The only network requests go to the sites you download from, the GPU/encoder catalog, and update checks.

Is it free? Can I use it for protected content?

It is free and open source under the MIT license. Only use it for content you own the rights to or that the platform allows you to download: you are responsible for your use.
