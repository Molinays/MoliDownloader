First steps: how to download any video Basics
How do I download a video in 3 seconds?

Downloading with MoliDowloader is very easy:

  1. Copy the video link from your browser or smartphone (e.g. from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Twitch, Facebook, etc.).
  2. Open MoliDowloader and paste the link into the box at the top.
  3. Choose whether you want the Video (MP4) or the Audio (MP3) and click Download.
Tip: As soon as you paste the link, MoliDowloader automatically recognizes which site it comes from and sets the best quality available, up to 4K!
Which sites are supported?

MoliDowloader supports over 1000 websites thanks to the integrated yt-dlp engine. Among the most popular:

  • Video and Social: YouTube, TikTok (without watermark), Instagram Reels and Posts, X / Twitter, Facebook Video, Reddit, Twitch, Vimeo, Dailymotion.
  • Music and Podcasts: Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube Music, Mixcloud, Bandcamp.
  • Streaming Platforms: Sites with embedded video players and protected HLS streaming (e.g. StreamingCommunity, VixCloud and similar).
Can I download multiple videos at the same time?

Absolutely! Switch to Advanced mode (via the button in the top right) and you can paste multiple links together (one per line). The app will manage the queue, downloading up to 8 in parallel at the maximum speed allowed by your connection.

Downloading music only (MP3) and entire Playlists Audio
How do I download only the audio in MP3 to listen on my phone or in the car?

Paste the link (for example a YouTube music video or a SoundCloud track) and select the Audio only (MP3) button.
MoliDowloader will extract the pure sound track, convert the file to high-fidelity MP3, and automatically embed the cover art and artist name into the track.

How does downloading an entire Playlist work?

If you paste a playlist link (from YouTube, Spotify, or SoundCloud):

  • The app will recognize that it is a playlist and show the Playlist badge.
  • All tracks or videos in the playlist will be downloaded automatically one after another.
  • No mess: The files will be neatly grouped inside a folder named after the playlist!
What happens if I download a music playlist while selecting "Video"?

The app will show a friendly reminder suggesting you download in Audio (MP3) format, which takes up much less space and is designed for music. If you still prefer the video format with images, you can confirm and download as video.

Protected sites, movies, and TV series Advanced
How does MoliDowloader download from protected sites like StreamingCommunity?

Many movie and TV sites use protected players (like VixCloud or hidden iframes) that try to hide the real video file from normal users.

MoliDowloader includes an intelligent proprietary resolver: it analyzes the page, follows the player's internal addresses, and traces the real HLS master stream. You do not need to install browser extensions like FetchV or The Stream Detector: the app does everything in complete transparency.

Legal reminder: Only download content you own the rights to or that is legally distributed for offline viewing.
What should I do if a video requires login or account access?

If content is visible only to subscribers (e.g. private videos on Facebook, Instagram, or age-restricted content on YouTube):

  • In MoliDowloader's settings you can enable the Browser Cookie Fallback option.
  • The app will temporarily read the session cookies of the browser already open on your PC (e.g. Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave) to download the authorized video, without making you re-enter passwords or credentials.
Trimming, editing, and modifying clips Editor
How can I trim a segment from a downloaded video?

You do not need Premiere or CapCut for a quick edit:

  1. Open the Editor tab at the top.
  2. Drag in the video you just downloaded (or a file already on your PC).
  3. Use the timeline sliders to choose the Start and End points.
  4. Press the Export button to save the trimmed video instantly!
Can I merge multiple videos or add a music track?

Yes! In the Editor timeline you can add multiple different clips, drag them to set the order, and click Add Music to insert a background song while adjusting its volume.

Can I export in vertical format for TikTok or Instagram Reels?

Absolutely. In the Editor's dropdown menu you can select the Vertical 9:16 format: the app will fit your horizontal video to the smartphone format without distortion.

Compressing videos and making them lighter with your graphics card GPU
How do I shrink a video to send it on Discord or WhatsApp?

It often happens that you have a 1 GB video to send in a chat or by email where there is a 25 MB or 10 MB limit.

  1. Go to the Compress tab.
  2. Drag in the video you want to lighten.
  3. Choose Target Size and enter the desired megabytes (for example 24 MB to fit on Discord).
  4. Click Start Compression: MoliDowloader will mathematically calculate the perfect bitrate to respect the limit while maintaining the highest possible visual quality.
What is GPU acceleration (NVIDIA NVENC, Intel QSV, AMD AMF)?

Normal programs use the processor (CPU) to compress videos, causing it to overheat and taking a long time. MoliDowloader automatically detects your computer's graphics card (NVIDIA GeForce, AMD Radeon, or Intel integrated graphics) and uses the dedicated hardware chips to complete compression up to 4 times faster.

Versions, Installation, and Updates System
What is the difference between the "Installer (Setup)" and "Portable" versions?

Both have exactly the same features:

  • Installer version: Installs with a single click (no boring configuration windows), creates the desktop and Start menu icon, and updates itself automatically whenever a new version is released.
  • Portable version: A single .exe file that installs nothing. You can put it anywhere (even on a USB drive). It does not touch the Windows registry. When an update comes out, just re-download the executable and replace the old file.
How does automatic updating of the installed version work?

You will never see boring guided installation wizards again. When there is an update:
The app downloads it in the background. When it is ready, it notifies you and you can click Restart to update. The app runs a quick silent update screen and reopens updated in a few seconds!

Why does Windows show the blue "Windows protected your PC" screen (SmartScreen)?

This warning appears on Windows for any free open-source program that does not purchase an expensive annual commercial certificate (which costs hundreds or thousands of dollars a year from Microsoft).

How to proceed in complete safety:

  1. Click the "More info" text.
  2. Click the "Run anyway" button.

MoliDowloader is 100% transparent and safe: the entire source code is public and available on GitHub.

Privacy, Folders, and Security Privacy
Where are downloaded files saved on my computer?

To keep everything perfectly organized, MoliDowloader automatically creates its folders in your PC's Documents folder:

  • Documents\MoliDowloader\Download for downloaded videos and music tracks.
  • Documents\MoliDowloader\Montaggi for Editor exports.
  • Documents\MoliDowloader\Compressioni for compressed videos.

You can change any folder at any time from the Settings tab or directly from Easy mode!

Are my files or downloaded links sent to any external server?

Absolutely NOT. MoliDowloader is designed according to the principle of total privacy:

  • All processing, video conversion, audio extraction, and compression happen 100% offline on your processor and graphics card.
  • No personal data, history, or files are ever uploaded to external servers or the cloud.
  • No advertising, no tracking, or hidden telemetry.
Is MoliDowloader free?

Yes, MoliDowloader is completely free and open source under the MIT license. There are no subscriptions, no paid "pro" versions, and no artificial download limits.
